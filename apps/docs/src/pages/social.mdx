# Social stack

Stage: `prep_weeks_out`, then `grow_x`.

Eyes equal buys. The curve can be fair and still invisible. The year-one habit is to show up on X and Telegram with the same honesty as the launch post.

## Telegram

`social_telegram_plan` splits the job:

- An announcement channel for the named launch time, the fair-launch post, and later the lock proofs.
- A chat for questions. Mods do not promise a price.
- A pin that states the dump caveat and that snipers are common.

## X Community

`social_x_community_plan` and `prep_x_checklist` cover the public surface:

- Open the community before launch week.
- Post the launch time in the community and on the profile.
- X Premium so the posts have a chance of being seen.
- An AMA (`grow_x_ama_plan`) that starts with the curve, the 5% dev buy, and the dump caveat.

## X Developer API

`grow_x_dev_api_checklist` tracks whether an app exists and which reads or posts you intend. It has no key field. Changing credentials is `admin_x_credentials_change`, which is a stub with a guardian acknowledgement and still accepts no secret.

## Boosts

Fee-funded boosts are attention, not price support. `ads_preview` and `grow_x_boost_plan` describe them. `ads_submit` requires `confirm: true` and spends nothing in Phase 1.
