# The dump after graduation

Say this before the launch, and say it again before graduation.

On a public bonding curve, **snipers and bundlers are common**. Other people will try to buy in the same seconds you do. This project does not invite them, and this repository does not include bots that snipe or bundle.

**A dump after graduation is typical.** People who bought the curve often sell when the liquidity moves to a pool. The price can fall hard. A buyer who was not told that is being blindsided. The fair-launch post exists so they are not.

Tools:

- `launch_curve_build_announcement` writes the warning into the post.
- `graduate_checklist` repeats it on migrate day.
- `resume` can store the acknowledgements `dumpAfterGraduationTypical` and `snipersAndBundlersCommon` on the local session.

What this is not: a promise that the price recovers, and not a plan to “defend” the candle with a buyback or a burn. Fees on the locked Meteora pool compound into quote token B instead. See [Meteora](/meteora).
